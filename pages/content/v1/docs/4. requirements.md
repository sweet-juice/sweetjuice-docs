## Prerequisites

- Go 1.24+
- Android SDK and Android NDK installed (Android Studio recommended)
- `xtool.sh` (For cross-platform iOS builds)
- `git`, `curl`
 
 **NB**: For less mess, do not alter the default location for Android SDK.
